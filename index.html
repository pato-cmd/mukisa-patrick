About Mukisa Patrick

Mukisa Patrick is a software engineering student and technology entrepreneur based in Uganda, with an interest in developing practical software solutions and building technology-driven businesses.

He is pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering at Ndejje University and has also undertaken networking studies covering routing and system administration through Cisco Networking Academy.

Beyond building software, Patrick is interested in technology entrepreneurship, continuous learning, and developing digital products that can grow into sustainable businesses. His long-term ambition is to strengthen his expertise in software engineering, deliver useful technology solutions, and build Mofic Technologies into a recognised and trusted technology brand.
